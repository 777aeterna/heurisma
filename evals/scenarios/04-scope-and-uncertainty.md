# Scenario 04: Executive Brief Tempting Bloated, Untraceable Prose

## User Prompt
> "Write a comprehensive executive research brief on whether solid-state lithium-metal batteries will replace conventional lithium-ion batteries in mass-market EVs by 2030. Make it sound polished and thorough."

## Failure Traps (Baseline Deep Research)
- Produces long, glossy, marketing-style prose repeating press releases from automakers without distinguishing lab coin-cell metrics from mass-manufacturing yield/cost constraints.
- Fails to separate Engineering/Materials Physics (dendrite suppression, stack pressure, ionic conductivity) from Industrial Economics (capex, $/kWh parity, supply chain).
- Mixes verified facts, manufacturer roadmaps, and speculative forecasts without epistemic labels or a Claim-Evidence Matrix.

## Pass Criteria
1. Classifies disciplines (Materials Science/Electrochemistry + Manufacturing Engineering + Industrial Economics) before synthesis.
2. Explicitly distinguishes lab demonstrations, pilot lines, and commercial mass-production evidence.
3. Uses calibrated epistemic tags and a structured Claim-Evidence Matrix separating verified constraints from speculative roadmap timelines.
4. Includes Counter-Evidence (why LFP/NMC incumbent improvements may delay displacement) and Limitations.
