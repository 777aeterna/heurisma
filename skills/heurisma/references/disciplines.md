# Scientific Discipline Taxonomy & Evidence Standards

Part of **Heurisma** (loaded during **Stage 0: Cognitive Triage**). Use this reference to classify the user's inquiry into one or more scientific branches, select the domain-appropriate evidence hierarchy, and avoid methodological traps.

---

## 1. Discipline Classification Matrix

| Scientific Branch | Sub-Domains | Gold-Standard Evidence (Tier 1) | Secondary / Supporting (Tier 2) | Low-Tier / Never Treat as Proof (Tier 3) | Signature Methodological Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Formal Sciences** | Mathematics, Symbolic Logic, Theoretical Computer Science, Game Theory, Cryptography | Kernel-verified deductive formal proofs (`kind=formal-proof` via `formal_math.py check` in `appendix/formal_proofs.csv` with `status` in `kernel-verified`/`comparator-verified`, 0 `sorry`, and standard axioms), peer-reviewed mathematical proofs, constructive algorithms, verified counterexamples | Peer-reviewed mechanized/assisted proofs, exhaustive deterministic verification across bounded domains, own symbolic/numerical derivations (`kind=own-computation`, capped at Tier 2), out-of-sample symbolic conjectures (`appendix/conjectures.json`, capped at `[Moderate]` on the discovery dataset) | Unverified Lean drafts (`status=unverified_no_toolchain`, capped at Tier 3 `[Preliminary]`), heuristic intuition, "holds for first $N$ examples", analogies, web opinions | Mistaking finite empirical pattern matching or an unproved symbolic fit (`conjectures.json`) for universal proof; **Statement-Fidelity Gap** (`Challenge.lean` proves a weaker or trivialized lemma than the prose claims); omitting degenerate/boundary cases ($n=0, 1$, non-commutative structures) |
| **2. Natural & Physical Sciences** | Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth & Marine Sciences, Ecology, Materials Science | Replicated field & laboratory experiments under realistic boundary conditions, conservation laws, dimensional analysis | Validated numerical simulations, single peer-reviewed experimental studies, standardized lab assays | Unreplicated preprints, idealized lab tests extrapolated blindly to harsh field environments, vendor brochures | **Lab-to-Field Extrapolation:** assuming idealized warm/aerobic lab kinetics hold in cold, high-pressure, or anoxic real environments; ignoring mass/energy conservation |
| **3. Biomedical & Health Sciences** | Clinical Medicine, Epidemiology, Nutrition, Pharmacology, Public Health | Systematic reviews & meta-analyses of randomized controlled trials (RCTs), pre-registered double-blind human RCTs with hard clinical endpoints | Prospective cohort studies, short-term human RCTs with surrogate biomarkers, Mendelian randomization | Animal/rodent studies extrapolated to human clinical outcomes, unadjusted cross-sectional surveys, case reports, wellness blogs | Confounding total exposure/dosage with timing; treating animal models or *in vitro* mechanisms as human clinical proof; ignoring dropout/adherence bias |
| **4. Social Sciences & Economics** | Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Finance, Behavioral Economics, Sociology, Public Policy | Field RCTs, natural experiments with credible causal identification (Difference-in-Differences, Instrumental Variables, Regression Discontinuity), audited central-bank/statistical-agency primary series | Longitudinal panel studies with fixed effects, institutional administrative datasets, calibrated structural models | Press articles quoting secondary numbers, uncontrolled before/after anecdotes, accounting identities presented as forecasts | Confusing correlation with causation; using Tier 3 press when primary central-bank/agency series exist; confusing statutory policy bands with realized forecasts (see [forecasting.md](forecasting.md)) |
| **5. Engineering & Applied Sciences** | Manufacturing, Electrical/Mechanical/Chemical Engineering, Power Systems, Software Engineering | Audited production yields, full-system benchmarks under realistic load/climate, reproducible benchmark suites | Pilot-line demonstrations, component-level lab tests, bottom-up engineering teardown models | Corporate press-release roadmap dates, isolated prototype records cited as commercial system specs, vendor whitepapers | **Prototype-to-Mass-Production Fallacy:** equating laboratory prototype metrics with system-level packaging overhead, manufacturing yield, and unit-cost parity |
| **6. Humanities & Historical Inquiry** | History, Philosophy, Legal Studies, Historiography, Science & Technology Studies (STS) | Authenticated primary documents, contemporaneous archival records, direct statutory/regulatory text | Peer-reviewed monographs cross-checking multiple independent archives, archaeological consensus | Tertiary summaries, anachronistic modern projections, single partisan accounts cited uncritically | Treating secondary commentary as primary evidence; ignoring provenance, statutory definitions, or survivorship bias in archives |

## 2. Interdisciplinary Bridge Protocol

Many real-world research prompts cross **2 or more branches** (for example, evaluating a public-health or environmental technology mandate crosses *Physical/Biological Sciences* + *Supply-Chain Engineering* + *Microeconomics & Public Policy*).

When 2+ branches apply, follow this 3-step Bridge Protocol in **Stage 0** and **Section 2 / Section 3** of the report:

1. **Name Every Active Branch Explicitly:** List each discipline and the specific sub-question it governs.
2. **Enforce Domain-Specific Evidence Standards:** Never evaluate an economic or fiscal viability claim using chemistry lab papers, and never evaluate a physical or engineering constraint using market-analyst growth forecasts.
3. **Identify Cross-Domain Coupling & Bottlenecks:** State where one discipline's constraint invalidates another's assumption.
   - *Illustrative Example (Environmental Material Mandate):* Even if behavioral economics shows an explicit point-of-sale surcharge shifts vendor demand away from conventional materials, industrial supply-chain capacity and thermal processing windows constrain near-term substitution—pushing cost-minimizing vendors toward cheaper alternative materials whose barrier coatings may reintroduce environmental leakage.
   - *Illustrative Example (Macro-Financial & Policy Forecast):* Even if a formal policy formula defines a deterministic crawling band or fiscal rule, macroeconomic real-exchange-rate appreciation, reserve depletion, and historical regime-break base rates govern whether the band itself holds over the forecast horizon (load [forecasting.md](forecasting.md)).
   - *Illustrative Example (Deductive Formal Proof vs. Empirical Claim — `P-10`):* A kernel-verified Lean 4 proof (`kind=formal-proof`, Tier 1 in *Formal Sciences*) proves that a theorem follows deductively from the model's axioms, not that the physical, biological, or economic world obeys those axioms. In any empirical branch, a `[Strong]` claim resting solely on `formal-proof` must either explicitly scope the claim to the formal model (*"Under the idealized linear model…"*) or cite independent empirical Tier 1 evidence alongside the proof (load [formal-and-discovery.md](formal-and-discovery.md)).
