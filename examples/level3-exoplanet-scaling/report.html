Empirical Two-Body Keplerian Scaling and Mass–Radius Regime Breakdown Across Confirmed Transiting Exoplanets

As-Of Date: 2026-11-27 | Depth Level: Level 3 | Primary Disciplines: Natural & Physical Sciences (Astrophysics & Planetary Dynamics); Formal Sciences (Symbolic Discovery & Deductive Verification)

Abstract

Across 1244 confirmed transiting exoplanets, observational data empirically recover two-body Keplerian orbital scaling out-of-sample (log holdout R² = 0.9834) and independently via transit geometry (log R² = 0.9871) [1], [2], [3] [strong]. Conversely, a single unbroken power law for the planetary mass–radius relation is decisively falsified by the Jovian degeneracy plateau (slope = −0.023; piecewise ΔBIC = 553.2) [1], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8], [2] [strong].

Falsifier: Finding a statistically significant drift in Π₁ across stellar mass strata (Kruskal-Wallis p < 0.001, |ρ| > 0.15) on independent transit-geometry samples, or observing a positive mass–radius slope (β > 0.20, R² > 0.30) in gas giants above 100 M_⊕, would overturn these conclusions.

4. Findings

4.1 Buckingham Π Dimensional Analysis & Symbolic Discovery

Exact rational dimensional analysis (conjecture.py units) over (P_s [s], a_m [m], GM [m³/s²], M_p [kg]) yields a single dimensionless group Π₁ = P_s · a_m−3/2 · GM1/2 and automatically prunes planetary mass M_p with exact exponent 0 [1], [9] [moderate]. On the real observational dataset (train n = 933, tail holdout n = 311 sorted by a_m), conjecture.py discover evaluates 29 candidate models and selects the dimensionally consistent Buckingham Π monomial C_LAW_1: P_s = 6.2502 * a_m^(3/2) * GM^(-1/2) [1], [9] [moderate]. Its fitted prefactor differs from exact 2π = 6.2832 by 0.52%, reduces training BIC from 23210.5 (null) to 18800.9, and achieves linear tail holdout R² = 0.9932 (log₁₀ holdout R² = 0.9834, relative holdout RMSE = 7.72%) [1], [9], [2] [moderate]. Note that unconstrained 4-parameter C_LAW_2 achieves virtually identical validation RMSE (23,577.95 s vs. 23,652.11 s, within 0.3%), so C_LAW_1 is selected by BIC parsimony (18800.9 vs. 18811.43) and Buckingham Π dimensional consistency.

The conservation-invariant search (conjecture.py discover --target __invariant__) independently recovers C_INV_1: P_s * a_m^(-3/2) * GM^(1/2) = 6.2764 (0.11% from 2π), with tail holdout R² = 0.9945 and relative-SD holdout RMSE = 0.0739 [1], [9] [moderate].

4.2 Out-of-Sample Keplerian Scaling, Transit-Geometry Control & Stratification

Across all 1244 transiting exoplanets orbiting 1029 stars, K = P_s² G M_⋆ / a_m³ has median 39.463 (IQR 0.2271) against 4π² = 39.478, while Π₁ has median 6.2819 (IQR 0.0181) and median relative period residual −0.02% (IQR 0.288%) [1], [2] [strong]. To eliminate potential circularity from archival pl_orbsmax values imputed via Kepler's Third Law, we test the n = 863 systems with independent transit-geometry ratios pl_ratdor (a/R_⋆) and stellar radii st_rad: using a_tr = (a/R_⋆) R_⋆ recovers log₁₀ holdout R² = 0.9871, median Π₁ = 6.2783, and median residual −0.078% (IQR 0.9074%) [1], [2], [3] [strong].

Stratifying by host stellar mass confirms invariance of Π₁ across M-dwarfs, K/G-dwarfs, and F/A-stars: Kruskal-Wallis H = 2.29 (permutation p = 0.3207), Spearman ρ(M_⋆, Π₁) = −0.0366 (p = 0.1959), and one-planet-per-star sensitivity (n = 1029) yields ρ = −0.0321 (p = 0.3033) [1], [2] [strong]. Across n = 1,093 systems with reported eccentricities, |rel_res| shows no monotonic inflation with eccentricity (Spearman ρ = −0.0555, p = 0.0655), whereas the n = 151 systems missing eccentricity show slightly wider dispersion (IQR 1.6797% vs. 0.5064% for e > 0.1) [1], [2] [strong].

4.3 Formal Deductive Verification Scope

Under the idealized integer-ratio algebraic model in appendix/formal/kepler-scaling/Challenge.lean, kepler_two_body_scaling proves that for integers sharing a common proportionality constant k ≠ 0, integer-multiple scaling of stellar mass (GM1 = s * GM2) and semi-major axis (a1 = m * a2) yields P1 * P1 * s = P2 * P2 * (m * m * m) [10] [strong]. Note that this formal theorem is explicitly scoped to the idealized integer ring identity (it does not formalize real-valued continuity or fractional exponents 3/2, −1/2, and hGM is unused in Solution.lean); formal_math.py check verified it against Lean v4.16.0 (status = kernel-verified, verification_mode = kernel-defeq, sorries = 0, axioms = ['propext'], Tier 1).

5. Counter-Evidence & Alternative Explanations

5.1 Planetary Mass–Radius Regime Breakdown

In stark contrast to the scale-invariant Keplerian law, the planetary mass–radius relation R_p(M_p) breaks single-power-law scaling across astrophysical composition regimes [1], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8], [2] [strong]. A global single power law across all 1244 planets yields slope 0.373 (95% CI [0.3593, 0.3882], R² = 0.737, RMSE = 0.2022 dex, BIC = −3962.5). Partitioning into rocky (< 2 M_⊕), sub-Neptune (2–100 M_⊕), and Jovian (> 100 M_⊕) regimes reveals a decisive turnover: while the rocky-to-sub-Neptune slope difference (0.2817 vs. 0.5383, permutation p = 0.003) has overlapping bootstrap CIs due to small rocky sample size (n = 31), the Jovian regime (> 100 M_⊕, n = 625) collapses to slope −0.023 (host-clustered 95% CI [−0.0573, 0.0089], R² = 0.004, Spearman ρ = −0.0012, p = 0.9725) [1], [4], [2] [strong]. Fitting a continuous 3-regime broken power law (4 parameters, knots at 2 and 100 M_⊕) yields slopes (0.2319, 0.6053, 0.0355), R² = 0.833, and ΔBIC = 553.2 (knot sensitivity at 120 M_⊕ gives Jovian slope 0.0078, BIC = −4544.23), while 5-fold out-of-sample CV RMSE improves from 0.2028 to 0.1617 dex [1], [4], [8], [2] [strong]. Abstract-only evidence from Otegi et al. (2020) similarly reports a break below 120 M_⊕ [11] [preliminary].

5.2 Outliers, Mass-Provenance Sensitivity, and Confounders

In the Keplerian residual audit, 107 systems (8.6%) exhibit |rel_res| > 5%. Joining the extended error columns ([@C10]) shows that 77 of 102 outliers with reported uncertainties (75.5%) lie within 3σ propagated observational error of pl_orbsmax and st_mass, and applying the two-body reduced-mass correction (M_⋆ + M_p) improves residuals for 70 of the 107 outliers [1], [2], [3] [strong]. In the mass–radius analysis, restricting to pl_bmassprov == "Mass" preserves 30/31 rocky, 585/588 sub-Neptune, and 625/625 Jovian planets with virtually identical slopes (0.2857, 0.5386, −0.023), ruling out Msini projection bias [1], [2] [strong].

def quantile(sorted_vals: Sequence[float], q: float) -> float:
    n = len(sorted_vals)
    if n == 0:
        raise ValueError("empty sequence")
    if n == 1:
        return sorted_vals[0]
    pos = q * (n - 1)
    lo = int(math.floor(pos))
    hi = int(math.ceil(pos))
    frac = pos - lo
    return sorted_vals[lo] * (1.0 - frac) + sorted_vals[hi] * frac


def robust_stats(vals: Sequence[float]) -> dict[str, float]:
    s = sorted(vals)
    med = statistics.median(s)
    q25 = quantile(s, 0.25)
    q75 = quantile(s, 0.75)
    iqr = q75 - q25
    mad = statistics.median(sorted(abs(v - med) for v in s))
    mean = statistics.fmean(s)
    sd = statistics.stdev(s) if len(s) > 1 else 0.0
    return {
        "n": float(len(s)),
        "mean": mean,
        "sd": sd,
        "median": med,
        "q25": q25,
        "q75": q75,
        "iqr": iqr,
        "mad": mad,
    }


def ols_simple(x: Sequence[float], y: Sequence[float]) -> dict[str, float]:
    n = len(x)
    mx = statistics.fmean(x)
    my = statistics.fmean(y)
    sxx = sum((xi - mx) ** 2 for xi in x)
    sxy = sum((xi - mx) * (yi - my) for xi, yi in zip(x, y))
    syy = sum((yi - my) ** 2 for yi in y)
    slope = sxy / sxx
    intercept = my - slope * mx
    rss = sum((yi - (intercept + slope * xi)) ** 2 for xi, yi in zip(x, y))
    r2 = 1.0 - rss / syy if syy > 0 else 0.0
    rmse = math.sqrt(rss / n)
    se_slope = math.sqrt((rss / max(n - 2, 1)) / sxx) if n > 2 and sxx > 0 else 0.0
    bic = n * math.log(max(rss / n, 1e-300)) + 2.0 * math.log(n)
    return {
        "n": float(n),
        "slope": slope,
        "intercept": intercept,
        "se_slope": se_slope,
        "r2": r2,
        "rmse": rmse,
        "rss": rss,
        "bic": bic,
    }


def solve_linear_system(a: list[list[float]], b: list[float]) -> list[float]:
    m = len(b)
    mat = [row[:] + [b[i]] for i, row in enumerate(a)]
    for col in range(m):
        piv = max(range(col, m), key=lambda r: abs(mat[r][col]))
        mat[col], mat[piv] = mat[piv], mat[col]
        denom = mat[col][col]
        if abs(denom) < 1e-14:
            raise ZeroDivisionError("singular matrix")
        for j in range(col, m + 1):
            mat[col][j] /= denom
        for r in range(m):
            if r != col and mat[r][col] != 0.0:
                factor = mat[r][col]
                for j in range(col, m + 1):
                    mat[r][j] -= factor * mat[col][j]
    return [mat[i][m] for i in range(m)]


def fit_continuous_broken_3(
    x: Sequence[float], y: Sequence[float], k1: float, k2: float
) -> dict[str, object]:
    """Continuous 3-regime piecewise linear fit: y = b0 + b1*x + d2*max(0, x-k1) + d3*max(0, x-k2)."""
    n = len(x)
    xtx = [[0.0] * 4 for _ in range(4)]
    xty = [0.0] * 4
    for xi, yi in zip(x, y):
        row = [1.0, xi, max(0.0, xi - k1), max(0.0, xi - k2)]
        for i in range(4):
            xty[i] += row[i] * yi
            for j in range(4):
                xtx[i][j] += row[i] * row[j]
    beta = solve_linear_system(xtx, xty)
    preds = [
        beta[0] + beta[1] * xi + beta[2] * max(0.0, xi - k1) + beta[3] * max(0.0, xi - k2)
        for xi in x
    ]
    rss = sum((yi - pi) ** 2 for yi, pi in zip(y, preds))
    my = statistics.fmean(y)
    syy = sum((yi - my) ** 2 for yi in y)
    r2 = 1.0 - rss / syy if syy > 0 else 0.0
    rmse = math.sqrt(rss / n)
    bic = n * math.log(max(rss / n, 1e-300)) + 4.0 * math.log(n)
    return {
        "beta": beta,
        "slopes": (beta[1], beta[1] + beta[2], beta[1] + beta[2] + beta[3]),
        "rss": rss,
        "r2": r2,
        "rmse": rmse,
        "bic": bic,
    }


def pearson_r(x: Sequence[float], y: Sequence[float]) -> float:
    mx = statistics.fmean(x)
    my = statistics.fmean(y)
    num = sum((a - mx) * (b - my) for a, b in zip(x, y))
    den = math.sqrt(sum((a - mx) ** 2 for a in x) * sum((b - my) ** 2 for b in y))
    return num / den if den > 0 else 0.0


def _rank(vals: Sequence[float]) -> list[float]:
    indexed = sorted(enumerate(vals), key=lambda t: t[1])
    ranks = [0.0] * len(vals)
    i = 0
    while i < len(indexed):
        j = i + 1
        while j < len(indexed) and indexed[j][1] == indexed[i][1]:
            j += 1
        avg_rank = 0.5 * (i + j - 1) + 1.0
        for k in range(i, j):
            ranks[indexed[k][0]] = avg_rank
        i = j
    return ranks


def spearman_rho(x: Sequence[float], y: Sequence[float]) -> float:
    return pearson_r(_rank(x), _rank(y))


def kruskal_wallis_stat(groups: Sequence[Sequence[float]]) -> float:
    pooled = [v for g in groups for v in g]
    ranks = _rank(pooled)
    n_tot = len(pooled)
    pos = 0
    h_num = 0.0
    for g in groups:
        ng = len(g)
        r_bar = statistics.fmean(ranks[pos : pos + ng])
        h_num += ng * ((r_bar - 0.5 * (n_tot + 1.0)) ** 2)
        pos += ng
    return (12.0 / (n_tot * (n_tot + 1.0))) * h_num


def kruskal_wallis_perm(groups: Sequence[Sequence[float]], n_perm: int = 1000, seed: int = 47) -> tuple[float, float]:
    obs_h = kruskal_wallis_stat(groups)
    pooled = [v for g in groups for v in g]
    sizes = [len(g) for g in groups]
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    ge = 0
    for _ in range(n_perm):
        rng.shuffle(pooled)
        perm_groups = []
        pos = 0
        for sz in sizes:
            perm_groups.append(pooled[pos : pos + sz])
            pos += sz
        if kruskal_wallis_stat(perm_groups) >= obs_h:
            ge += 1
    return obs_h, (ge + 1.0) / (n_perm + 1.0)


def bootstrap_ci(
    x: Sequence[float],
    y: Sequence[float],
    stat_fn,
    n_boot: int = 1000,
    seed: int = 42,
) -> tuple[float, float]:
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    n = len(x)
    boots = []
    for _ in range(n_boot):
        idx = [rng.randrange(n) for _ in range(n)]
        bx = [x[i] for i in idx]
        by = [y[i] for i in idx]
        try:
            boots.append(stat_fn(bx, by))
        except ZeroDivisionError:
            continue
    boots.sort()
    return quantile(boots, 0.025), quantile(boots, 0.975)


def cluster_bootstrap_slope(
    clusters: Sequence[Sequence[int]],
    x: Sequence[float],
    y: Sequence[float],
    n_boot: int = 1000,
    seed: int = 48,
) -> tuple[float, float]:
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    nc = len(clusters)
    boots = []
    for _ in range(n_boot):
        chosen = [clusters[rng.randrange(nc)] for _ in range(nc)]
        bx = [x[i] for cl in chosen for i in cl]
        by = [y[i] for cl in chosen for i in cl]
        if len(bx) > 3:
            try:
                boots.append(ols_simple(bx, by)["slope"])
            except ZeroDivisionError:
                continue
    boots.sort()
    return quantile(boots, 0.025), quantile(boots, 0.975)


def perm_pvalue_corr(
    x: Sequence[float],
    y: Sequence[float],
    n_perm: int = 2000,
    seed: int = 42,
) -> float:
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    obs = abs(spearman_rho(x, y))
    y_copy = list(y)
    ge = 0
    for _ in range(n_perm):
        rng.shuffle(y_copy)
        if abs(spearman_rho(x, y_copy)) >= obs:
            ge += 1
    return (ge + 1.0) / (n_perm + 1.0)


def perm_pvalue_slope_diff(
    x1: Sequence[float], y1: Sequence[float], x2: Sequence[float], y2: Sequence[float], n_perm: int = 1000, seed: int = 49
) -> float:
    obs_diff = abs(ols_simple(x2, y2)["slope"] - ols_simple(x1, y1)["slope"])
    px = list(x1) + list(x2)
    py = list(y1) + list(y2)
    n1 = len(x1)
    idx = list(range(len(px)))
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    ge = 0
    for _ in range(n_perm):
        rng.shuffle(idx)
        g1 = idx[:n1]
        g2 = idx[n1:]
        try:
            s1 = ols_simple([px[i] for i in g1], [py[i] for i in g1])["slope"]
            s2 = ols_simple([px[i] for i in g2], [py[i] for i in g2])["slope"]
        except ZeroDivisionError:
            continue
        if abs(s2 - s1) >= obs_diff:
            ge += 1
    return (ge + 1.0) / (n_perm + 1.0)

def sha256_file(path: Path) -> str:
    """Return the full 64-hex SHA-256 digest of a file."""
    h = hashlib.sha256()
    with path.open("rb") as fh:
        for chunk in iter(lambda: fh.read(1 << 20), b""):
            h.update(chunk)
    return h.hexdigest()


def sha12(path: Path) -> str:
    """Return the first 12 hex characters of the SHA-256 digest of a file."""

def _cell_class(s: str) -> str:
    s = s.strip()
    if re.fullmatch(r"[+-]?\d+", s):
        return _INT
    if re.fullmatch(r"[+-]?\d+(?:\.\d+)?[eE][+-]?\d+", s):
        return _SCI
    m = re.fullmatch(r"[+-]?(\d+(?:\.\d{3})*),(\d+)", s)  # comma as the last separator
    if m:
        whole, frac = m.group(1), m.group(2)
        if "." in whole or len(frac) != 3 or len(whole) > 3:
            return _EU_CLEAR
        return _COMMA_AMBIG
    if re.fullmatch(r"[+-]?\d{1,3}(?:,\d{3})+\.\d+", s):
        return _US_CLEAR

        return _US_CLEAR

        return _EU_CLEAR
    if re.fullmatch(r"[+-]?\d{1,3}\.\d{3}", s):
        return _DOT_AMBIG

    return delim, headers, body, _analyze_csv_columns(headers, body, delim, keep_strings)


def read_csv(path: Path, keep_strings: Any = False) -> list[dict[str, Any]]:
    """Read a CSV/TSV file: delimiter sniffing, per-column decimal convention, UTF-8/latin-1 fallback.

    Each column is read as decimal-comma or decimal-point from its own cells (a file may hold both); a column that
    mixes the two raises ValueError instead of guessing, mostly-text columns and zero-padded identifiers stay text,
    and ``keep_strings`` (a list of column names, or True) forces text.
    """
    _, headers, body, cols = csv_columns(path, keep_strings)
    out: list[dict[str, Any]] = []
    for r in body:
        row: dict[str, Any] = {}
        for ci, name in enumerate(headers):
            raw = r[ci] if ci < len(r) else ""
            info = cols[name]
            if info["keep"]:
                row[name] = raw.strip()
            else:
                row[name] = _to_number(raw, bool(info["comma"]))
        out.append(row)
    return out


def _xlsx_sheets(z: zipfile.ZipFile, max_bytes: int) -> dict[str, str]:
    wb = ET.fromstring(_read_xlsx_xml(z, "xl/workbook.xml", max_bytes))
    rels = ET.fromstring(_read_xlsx_xml(z, "xl/_rels/workbook.xml.rels", max_bytes))
    target = {r.get("Id"): r.get("Target") for r in rels.findall("pr:Relationship", NS)}
    out: dict[str, str] = {}
    sheets_el = wb.find("m:sheets", NS)
    if sheets_el is None:
        return out
    for s in sheets_el:
        rid = s.get(f"{{{NS['r']}}}id")
        raw_t = (target.get(rid) or "").lstrip("/")
        name = s.get("name") or "Sheet1"
        out[name] = raw_t if raw_t.startswith("xl/") else f"xl/{raw_t}"
    return out


def _col_index(ref: str) -> int:
    m = re.match(r"[A-Z]+", ref or "")
    if not m:
        return 0
    n = 0
    for ch in m.group(0):
        n = n * 26 + ord(ch) - 64
    return n - 1


def read_xlsx(path: Path, sheet: str | None = None, max_rows: int | None = None) -> tuple[list[str], list[list[Any]]]:
    """Read an XLSX sheet without external dependencies; returns (sheet_names, rows)."""
    p = Path(path)
    _check_file_size(p)
    with zipfile.ZipFile(p) as z:
        max_uncompressed = _check_xlsx_zip(z)
        sheets = _xlsx_sheets(z, max_uncompressed)
        shared: list[str] = []
        if "xl/sharedStrings.xml" in z.namelist():
            ss_bytes = _read_xlsx_xml(z, "xl/sharedStrings.xml", max_uncompressed)
            for si in ET.fromstring(ss_bytes).findall("m:si", NS):
                shared.append("".join(t.text or "" for t in si.iter(f"{{{NS['m']}}}t")))
        name = sheet or next(iter(sheets))
        rows: list[list[Any]] = []
        sheet_bytes = _read_xlsx_xml(z, sheets[name], max_uncompressed)
        for _, el in ET.iterparse(io.BytesIO(sheet_bytes)):
            if el.tag != f"{{{NS['m']}}}row":
                continue
            vals: dict[int, Any] = {}
            for c in el.findall("m:c", NS):
                v = c.find("m:v", NS)
                t = c.get("t")
                if t == "inlineStr":
                    val: Any = "".join(x.text or "" for x in c.iter(f"{{{NS['m']}}}t"))
                elif v is None or v.text is None:
                    continue
                elif t == "s":
                    val = shared[int(v.text)]
                elif t in ("str", "e"):
                    val = v.text
                elif t == "b":
                    val = v.text == "1"
                else:
                    f = float(v.text)
                    val = int(f) if f.is_integer() else f
                vals[_col_index(c.get("r") or "A1")] = val
            if vals:
                rows.append([vals.get(i) for i in range(max(vals) + 1)])
            el.clear()
            if max_rows and len(rows) >= max_rows:
                break
    return list(sheets), rows


def excel_date(serial: float) -> date:
    """Convert an Excel serial date number (1900 date system) to a Python date."""
    return date.fromordinal(date(1899, 12, 30).toordinal() + int(serial))


def read_json(path: Path) -> Any:
    """Read and parse a UTF-8 JSON file."""
    p = Path(path)
    _check_file_size(p)
    return json.loads(p.read_text(encoding="utf-8"))


def guess_header_row(rows: list[list[Any]], scan: int = 30) -> int:
    """Return index of the first row with the highest number of non-empty text cells."""
    best, best_i = -1, 0
    for i, r in enumerate(rows[:scan]):
        n = sum(isinstance(v, str) and v.strip() != "" for v in r)
        if n > best:
            best, best_i = n, i
    return best_i


def read_table(
    path: str | Path, sheet: str | None = None, header_row: int | None = None, keep_strings: Any = False
) -> list[dict[str, Any]]:
    """Read CSV, TSV, XLSX, or JSON into a list of row dicts (auto-detects XLSX headers and drops footer notes)."""
    p = Path(path)
    kind = DATA_EXT.get(p.suffix.lower())
    if kind == "csv":
        return read_csv(p, keep_strings=keep_strings)
    if kind == "xlsx":
        _, rows = read_xlsx(p, sheet)
        if not rows:
            return []
        if header_row is None:
            header_row = guess_header_row(rows)
        head = [str(h).strip() if h is not None else f"col{i}" for i, h in enumerate(rows[header_row])]
        body = [r for r in rows[header_row + 1 :] if sum(v is not None and v != "" for v in r) >= 2]
        return [dict(zip(head, r)) for r in body]
    if kind == "json":
        js = read_json(p)
        if isinstance(js, dict):
            for key in ("results", "data", "rows", "items"):
                if isinstance(js.get(key), list):
                    return js[key]
            return [js]
        return js
    raise ValueError(f"Unsupported tabular format: {p.suffix} (pdf/xls/ods require conversion or an external reader)")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- preview
def preview(path: str | Path, n: int = 10, sheet: str | None = None, keep_strings: Any = False) -> str:
    """Return a concise human-readable preview of a local data file."""
    p = Path(path)
    kind = DATA_EXT.get(p.suffix.lower(), "unknown")
    out = [f"{p}  [{kind}, {p.stat().st_size:,} bytes, sha256 {sha12(p)}]"]
    if kind == "csv":
        rows = read_csv(p, keep_strings=keep_strings)
        if not rows:
            raise ValueError(f"{p}: file is empty or has no data rows")
        delim, headers, raw_rows, cols = csv_columns(p, keep_strings)
        out.append(f"rows={len(rows)} delimiter={delim!r}")
        out.append(f"columns={list(rows[0])}")
        out.append("detected format per column:")
        for name in headers:
            info = cols[name]
            note = " (zero-padded identifier kept as text)" if info.get("identifier") else ""
            out.append(f"  {name}: {info['format']}{note}")
        out.append("first rows (raw -> converted):")
        for raw, conv in list(zip(raw_rows, rows))[:n]:
            parts = []
            for ci, name in enumerate(headers):
                rv = (raw[ci] if ci < len(raw) else "").strip()
                cv = conv[name]
                parts.append(f"{name}={cv!r}" if str(cv) == rv else f"{name}: raw {rv!r} -> {cv!r}")
            out.append("  " + "; ".join(parts))
    elif kind == "xlsx":
        sheets, xlsx_rows = read_xlsx(p, sheet, max_rows=n)
        out.append(f"sheets={sheets}  (showing: {sheet or sheets[0]})")
        out.extend(" | ".join("" if v is None else str(v) for v in r)[:200] for r in xlsx_rows)
    elif kind == "json":
        try:
            js = read_json(p)
        except json.JSONDecodeError as exc:
            raise ValueError(f"{p}: invalid JSON ({exc})") from exc
        if isinstance(js, list):
            out.append(f"list of {len(js)} items")
            out.extend(json.dumps(x, ensure_ascii=False)[:200] for x in js[:n])
        elif isinstance(js, dict):
            out.append(f"object with keys {list(js)[:20]}")
            for k, v in list(js.items())[:n]:
                desc = f"list[{len(v)}]" if isinstance(v, list) else json.dumps(v, ensure_ascii=False)[:120]
                out.append(f"  {k}: {desc}")
        else:
            out.append(repr(js)[:200])
    elif kind == "text":
        out.extend(p.read_text(encoding="utf-8", errors="replace").splitlines()[:n])
    else:
        out.append("No stdlib reader available: file listed only (convert to CSV or use an external tool).")
    return "\n".join(out)

_LOCAL_LABEL: dict[str, str] = {"en": "local file", "es": "archivo local"}


def offline_enabled() -> bool:
    """True when HEURISMA_OFFLINE is set to 1/true/yes/on: skip the network and force the local-data rung."""
    return os.environ.get("HEURISMA_OFFLINE", "").strip().lower() in {"1", "true", "yes", "on"}


def _read_rows(csv_path: Path) -> list[dict[str, str]]:
    """Rows of a CSV as dicts ([] if the file does not exist); a byte-order mark saved by a spreadsheet is tolerated."""
    if not csv_path.is_file():
        return []
    with csv_path.open(encoding="utf-8-sig", newline="") as fh:
        return list(csv.DictReader(fh))


def utc_now() -> str:
    """ISO-8601 UTC timestamp (second precision) for ``data_attempts.csv`` ``attempted_utc``."""
    return datetime.now(timezone.utc).strftime("%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ")


def _ends_with_newline(path: Path) -> bool:
    with path.open("rb") as fh:
        fh.seek(0, 2)
        if fh.tell() == 0:
            return True
        fh.seek(-1, 2)
        return fh.read(1) in (b"\n", b"\r")


def _append_row(csv_path: Path, columns: tuple[str, ...], row: dict[str, str]) -> None:
    """Append ``row``. An existing file missing some of ``columns`` (e.g. a v0.5 ``data_attempts.csv`` without
    ``attempted_utc``) is widened first: its old rows get an empty value, so they read as undated/inherited."""
    csv_path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    is_new = not csv_path.exists() or csv_path.stat().st_size == 0
    if not is_new:
        with csv_path.open(encoding="utf-8-sig", newline="") as fh:
            reader = csv.DictReader(fh)
            header = [c.strip() for c in (reader.fieldnames or [])]
            old_rows = list(reader)
        missing = [c for c in columns if c not in header]
        if missing:
            header += missing
            with csv_path.open("w", newline="", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
                writer = csv.DictWriter(fh, fieldnames=header, extrasaction="ignore", restval="", lineterminator="\n")
                writer.writeheader()
                writer.writerows(old_rows)
        fieldnames = header
    else:
        fieldnames = list(columns)
    with csv_path.open("a", newline="", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
        writer = csv.DictWriter(fh, fieldnames=fieldnames, extrasaction="ignore", restval="", lineterminator="\n")
        if is_new:
            writer.writeheader()
        elif not _ends_with_newline(csv_path):
            fh.write("\n")  # a file saved by hand without a final newline: do not glue the new row to its last line
        writer.writerow(row)


def next_local_id(sources_csv: str | Path) -> str:
    """Return the next free local claim id (L1, L2, ...) for appendix/sources.csv."""
    used = [
        int(m.group(1))
        for r in _read_rows(Path(sources_csv))
        if (m := re.fullmatch(r"L(\d+)", (r.get("claim_id") or "").strip()))
    ]
    return f"L{max(used, default=0) + 1}"


def run_start(appendix_dir: str | Path, force: bool = False) -> dict[str, str]:
    """Stamp the run start in ``appendix/run.json`` (Stage 0). Idempotent: an existing stamp is returned unchanged
    unless ``force``; attempts logged before this instant count as inherited from an earlier run."""
    path = Path(appendix_dir) / "run.json"
    if path.is_file() and not force:
        try:
            data = json.loads(path.read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
            if isinstance(data, dict) and data.get("run_started_utc"):
                return data
        except json.JSONDecodeError:
            pass
    data = {"run_started_utc": utc_now(), "stamped_by": "local_data.py run-start"}
    path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    path.write_text(json.dumps(data, indent=1) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    return data


def append_attempt(
    attempts_csv: str | Path,

    fallback: str = "",

        "query_or_endpoint": query,
        "outcome": outcome,

def _find_registered(sources_csv: Path, url: str, sha: str) -> dict[str, str] | None:
    legacy = "file://" + url if not url.startswith(("file:", "http:", "https:")) else url  # v0.5 rows
    for r in _read_rows(sources_csv):
        if (r.get("url") or "").strip() in (url, legacy) or sha in (r.get("source_title") or "") or sha in (r.get("sha256") or ""):
            return r
    return None


def _relative_or_uri(p: Path, base: Path) -> str:
    """Return a quoted POSIX relative path under ``base``, or a ``file://`` URI if outside or on another drive."""
    try:
        rel = Path(os.path.relpath(p, base.resolve())).as_posix()
    except ValueError:
        return p.as_uri()
    return quote(rel) if not rel.startswith("../") else p.as_uri()


def register_local(
    path: str | Path,
    sources_csv: str | Path,
    tier: str,
    title: str,
    claim_id: str = "",
    data_as_of: str = "",
    lang: str = "en",
    relative_to: str | Path | None = None,
    kind: str = "data",
    selection: str = "user-curated",
    retrieved_utc: str = "unknown",
) -> dict[str, str]:
    """Append a local-file provenance row to sources.csv (project-relative path + SHA-256) and return it.

    - ``claim_id`` empty -> next free ``L<n>`` so the source can be cited as ``[@L1]``.
    - Registering the same file again (same URI or same SHA-256) returns the existing row, never a duplicate.
    - ``relative_to`` stores a scheme-less project-relative path (``appendix/data/x.csv``) for files inside that
      folder; ``file://path`` is kept only for files outside it (a ``file://...`` URI would read
      ``appendix`` as a host name).
    - ``lang`` (``en``/``es``) localizes the "(local file, sha256 ...)" suffix.
    - ``selection``: who chose the file (``user-curated`` by default for a file the user supplied, ``agent-fetched``,
      ``third-party``); ``retrieved_utc``: when it was obtained from its origin, or ``unknown`` (then the validator
      keeps a user-curated row at Tier 3).
    """
    p = Path(path).resolve()
    sha = sha256_file(p)
    url = _relative_or_uri(p, Path(relative_to)) if relative_to is not None else p.as_uri()
    if kind not in VALID_KINDS:
        raise ValueError(f"kind must be one of {', '.join(VALID_KINDS)}; got {kind!r}")
    if selection not in VALID_SELECTIONS:
        raise ValueError(f"selection must be one of {', '.join(VALID_SELECTIONS)}; got {selection!r}")
    sp = Path(sources_csv)
    existing = _find_registered(sp, url, sha)
    if existing is not None:
        return dict(existing)
    label = _LOCAL_LABEL.get(lang, _LOCAL_LABEL["en"])
    row = {
        "claim_id": claim_id or next_local_id(sp),
        "source_title": f"{title} ({label}, sha256 {sha})",
        "url": url,
        "tier": tier,
        "accessed_date": date.today().isoformat(),
        "data_as_of": data_as_of,
        "kind": kind,
        "sha256": sha,
        "selection": selection,
        "retrieved_utc": retrieved_utc or "unknown",
    }
    _append_row(sp, SOURCE_COLS, row)
    return row


def import_local(
    path: str | Path,
    into: str | Path,
    sources_csv: str | Path,
    tier: str,
    title: str,
    lang: str = "en",
    claim_id: str = "",
    data_as_of: str = "",
    selection: str = "user-curated",
    retrieved_utc: str = "unknown",
    kind: str = "data",
) -> dict[str, str]:
    """Copy a user file into ``into`` (appendix/data), register the COPY, and log the rung in data_attempts.csv.

    Idempotent: importing the same file again adds no rows. Raises ValueError if a different file of the
    same name already sits in ``into`` (nothing is overwritten).
    """
    src = Path(path).resolve()
    dest_dir = Path(into)
    dest_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    dest = (dest_dir / src.name).resolve()
    if dest.exists() and dest != src and sha256_file(dest) != sha256_file(src):
        raise ValueError(f"{dest} already exists with different content; rename the source file or remove the old copy")
    if not dest.exists():
        shutil.copy2(src, dest)
    sp = Path(sources_csv)
    report_root = sp.resolve().parent.parent
    sha = sha256_file(dest)
    rel_url = _relative_or_uri(dest, report_root)
    already = _find_registered(sp, rel_url, sha) is not None
    row = register_local(
        dest, sp, tier, title, claim_id=claim_id, data_as_of=data_as_of, lang=lang, relative_to=report_root,
        kind=kind, selection=selection, retrieved_utc=retrieved_utc,
    )
    if not already:
        append_attempt(
            sp.parent / "data_attempts.csv",
            title,
            f"{_LOCAL_LABEL.get(lang, _LOCAL_LABEL['en'])} ({src.name})",
            rel_url,
            "used",
            fallback=f"local:{rel_url} sha256:{sha}",
        )
    return row


TABLE_KINDS = ("csv", "xlsx", "json")


def _wishlist(path: str | Path | None) -> list[tuple[str, list[str]]]:
    """Lines ``name: keyword, keyword`` -> [(name, [keywords])]; blank lines and ``#`` comments are skipped."""
    if not path:
        return []
    items = []
    for line in Path(path).read_text(encoding="utf-8").splitlines():
        line = line.strip()
        if not line or line.startswith("#"):
            continue
        name, _, kws = line.partition(":")
        words = [w.strip().lower() for w in (kws or name).split(",") if w.strip()]
        items.append((name.strip(), words))
    return items


def intake(
    folder: str | Path,
    appendix: str | Path = "appendix",
    channel: str = "data",
    selection: str = "user-curated",
    wishlist: str | Path | None = None,
    tier: str = "Tier 3",
    retrieved_utc: str = "unknown",
    lang: str = "en",
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Pre-intake (Stage -1): inspect, import and register every file of ``folder`` in one pass, and write
    ``appendix/intake.md`` (accepted/rejected files, detected format per CSV column, gaps against a wish-list).

    ``channel="data"`` imports tables (CSV/TSV/XLSX/JSON) into ``appendix/data/`` as ``kind=data``; a CSV with an
    ambiguous decimal column is rejected, never guessed. ``channel="sources"`` copies documents into
    ``appendix/corpus/`` as ``kind=literature``. Every row records ``selection`` and ``retrieved_utc``.
    """
    if channel not in ("data", "sources"):
        raise ValueError("channel must be 'data' or 'sources'")
    if selection not in VALID_SELECTIONS:
        raise ValueError(f"selection must be one of {', '.join(VALID_SELECTIONS)}; got {selection!r}")
    folder, appendix = Path(folder), Path(appendix)
    sources_csv = appendix / "sources.csv"
    accepted: list[dict[str, Any]] = []
    rejected: list[tuple[str, str]] = []
    for f in sorted(p for p in folder.rglob("*") if p.is_file() and "__pycache__" not in p.parts):
        rel = f.relative_to(folder).as_posix()
        kind_of = DATA_EXT.get(f.suffix.lower(), "unknown")
        formats: dict[str, str] = {}
        if channel == "data":
            if kind_of not in TABLE_KINDS:
                rejected.append((rel, f"not a table ({kind_of}); use --channel sources for documents"))
                continue
            try:
                if kind_of == "csv":
                    _, headers, _, cols = csv_columns(f)
                    formats = {h: cols[h]["format"] for h in headers}
                else:
                    read_table(f)
            except (ValueError, KeyError, OSError, json.JSONDecodeError, zipfile.BadZipFile) as exc:
                rejected.append((rel, str(exc)))
                continue
            row = import_local(f, appendix / "data", sources_csv, tier, f.stem, lang=lang,
                               selection=selection, retrieved_utc=retrieved_utc)
        else:
            dest_dir = appendix / "corpus"
            dest_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
            dest = dest_dir / f.name
            if dest.exists() and sha256_file(dest) != sha256_file(f):
                rejected.append((rel, f"{dest} already exists with different content"))
                continue
            if not dest.exists():
                shutil.copy2(f, dest)
            row = register_local(dest, sources_csv, tier, f.stem, lang=lang, relative_to=appendix.resolve().parent,
                                 kind="literature", selection=selection, retrieved_utc=retrieved_utc)
        accepted.append({"file": rel, "claim_id": row["claim_id"], "sha256": row.get("sha256", ""), "formats": formats})

    gaps = []
    for name, words in _wishlist(wishlist):
        haystack = " ".join(
            [a["file"].lower() for a in accepted] + [c.lower() for a in accepted for c in a["formats"]]
        )
        if not any(w in haystack for w in words):
            gaps.append(name)

    section_header = f"## Channel: {channel} (`{folder.name}/`)"
    sec_lines = [
        section_header,
        "",
        f"- folder: `{folder.name}/` · channel: {channel} · selection: {selection} · retrieved_utc: {retrieved_utc}",
        f"- run at: {utc_now()} (local_data.py intake)",
        "",
        "### Files",
        "",
    ]
    for a in accepted:
        sec_lines.append(f"- accepted: {a['file']} -> [@{a['claim_id']}] (sha256 {a['sha256']})")
        for col, fmt in a["formats"].items():
            sec_lines.append(f"  - {col}: {fmt}")
    for rel, why in rejected:
        sec_lines.append(f"- rejected: {rel} — {why}")
    if wishlist:
        sec_lines += ["", "### Gaps (wish-list items no accepted file covers)", ""]
        sec_lines += [f"- {g}" for g in gaps] or ["- none"]
    sec_block = "\n".join(sec_lines)
    footer = "Uploading a file never raises its tier: without an origin URL, a retrieval date and a hash it stays Tier 3."
    appendix.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    intake_md = appendix / "intake.md"
    if intake_md.is_file():
        existing = intake_md.read_text(encoding="utf-8")
        body_no_footer = existing.replace(footer, "").strip()
        sec_re = re.compile(
            rf"^## Channel:\s*{re.escape(channel)}\b.*?(?=^## Channel:|\Z)",
            flags=re.MULTILINE | re.DOTALL,
        )
        if sec_re.search(body_no_footer):
            merged = sec_re.sub(sec_block + "\n\n", body_no_footer).strip()
        elif body_no_footer.startswith("# Intake report"):
            merged = body_no_footer + "\n\n" + sec_block
        else:
            merged = "# Intake report\n\n" + sec_block
        intake_md.write_text(merged + "\n\n" + footer + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    else:

def _prereg_digest(record: dict[str, Any]) -> str:
    keys = ("sealed_utc", "hypotheses", "plan", "known_artifacts", "post_hoc")
    body = {k: record[k] for k in keys if k in record}
    return hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(body, sort_keys=True, ensure_ascii=False).encode("utf-8")).hexdigest()


def preregister(
    appendix: str | Path,
    hypotheses: dict[str, str],
    plan: str,
    allow_post_hoc: bool = False,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Seal H0/H1/... and the analysis plan in ``appendix/preregistration.json`` before looking at the data.

    Refuses to overwrite an existing seal, records ``known_artifacts`` present in ``appendix/``, and refuses
    post-hoc sealing if ``output.txt`` or ``numbers.json`` already exist unless ``allow_post_hoc=True``.
    """
    app = Path(appendix)
    path = app / "preregistration.json"
    if path.exists():
        raise ValueError(f"{path} already exists; a preregistration is sealed once (explain any change in Section 3)")
    if not hypotheses.get("H0") or not hypotheses.get("H1"):
        raise ValueError("preregister needs at least H0 and H1")
    post_hoc_files = [name for name in ("output.txt", "numbers.json") if (app / name).is_file()]
    if post_hoc_files and not allow_post_hoc:
        raise ValueError(
            f"Cannot seal preregistration after analysis outputs already exist ({', '.join(post_hoc_files)}); "
            f"run prereg in Stage 1 before analysis.py (or pass --allow-post-hoc to mark it post-hoc)."
        )
    known_artifacts: dict[str, str] = {}
    if app.is_dir():
        for fp in sorted(app.rglob("*"), key=lambda p: p.relative_to(app).parts):
            if fp.is_file() and "__pycache__" not in fp.parts and fp.suffix != ".pyc":
                known_artifacts[fp.relative_to(app).as_posix()] = sha12(fp)
    record: dict[str, Any] = {
        "sealed_utc": utc_now(),
        "hypotheses": hypotheses,
        "plan": plan,
        "known_artifacts": known_artifacts,
    }
    if post_hoc_files:
        record["post_hoc"] = True
    record["sha256"] = _prereg_digest(record)
    path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    path.write_text(json.dumps(record, ensure_ascii=False, indent=1) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    return record
