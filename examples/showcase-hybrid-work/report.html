Hybrid Work (2 Days Remote) vs. Full-Time Office in Software Engineering: Output, Retention, and Seniority Heterogeneity

As-Of Date: 2026-11-20 | Depth Level: Level 2 (Empirical / Data Study) | Primary Disciplines: Social Sciences & Economics (Labor Economics) + Engineering & Applied Sciences (Empirical Software Engineering)

Abstract

A 2-day/week hybrid schedule sharply reduces 12-month engineering attrition by 29.0% (9.8% vs. 13.9%), consistent with peer-reviewed RCT evidence [strong], but pooled output averages mask opposite seniority effects [moderate]. In the local 16-site dataset (n = 590 engineers), hybrid work raises senior PR output by +2.73 pp while lowering junior PR output by -6.28 pp alongside a two-thirds drop in weekly mentoring hours [moderate].

Falsifier: Individual-level longitudinal telemetry controlling for defect severity and structured virtual-onboarding interventions showing junior engineers match office-based output and skill growth under hybrid schedules.

1. Executive Summary

  • What Would Change This Conclusion: Individual-level longitudinal telemetry controlling for defect severity and structured virtual-onboarding interventions showing junior engineers match office-based output and skill growth under hybrid schedules.
  • Key Takeaways:
    • Retention gain is robust: Local 12-month attrition drops by 29.0% [1], [2], closely matching the 33% quit-rate reduction in the 1,612-employee Trip.com Nature RCT [3] [strong].
    • Pooling bias hides seniority divergence: The pooled output difference (-1.78 pp) averages a positive senior gain (+2.73 pp) with a steep junior decline (-6.28 pp) [2] [moderate].
    • Mentoring proximity mechanism: Junior hybrid sites drop from 5.15 to 1.73 mentoring hours/week (r = +0.999 with output delta), aligning with co-location feedback findings in software engineering [4] [moderate].

2. Framing & Hypotheses

  • Scientific Discipline(s) & Depth Level: Social Sciences & Economics (Labor Economics — causal field experiments, retention dynamics) + Engineering & Applied Sciences (Empirical Software Engineering — PR throughput, code review mentoring) | Level 2. Labor Economics governs attrition identification and subgroup heterogeneity; Software Engineering governs output metric validity and mentoring bottlenecks.
  • Research Question: Compared to a 5-day office schedule, does a 2-day/week hybrid policy improve software-engineering PR output and 12-month retention, and is the effect homogeneous across senior and junior engineers?
  • Hypotheses:
    • H₀: A 2-day hybrid schedule produces no statistically distinguishable change in PR output or 12-month retention across seniority groups.
    • H₁: A 2-day hybrid schedule lowers 12-month attrition without reducing aggregate output, with uniform gains across seniority levels.
    • H₂: A 2-day hybrid schedule lowers attrition overall, but produces heterogeneous output effects—raising senior output while reducing junior output due to lower in-person mentoring proximity.
  • Refutation Criteria: H₁ is falsified if junior and senior output effects have opposite signs with non-overlapping 95% bootstrap confidence intervals. H₀ is falsified if 12-month attrition falls by ≥15% relative to office sites with a 95% CI excluding zero.

3. Methodology & Evidence Standards

  • Domain Evidence Hierarchy: Tier 1 = peer-reviewed randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and natural experiments with causal identification; Tier 2 = multi-site administrative/organizational RCT extracts with subgroup covariates; Tier 3 = uncontrolled surveys, press articles, or vendor whitepapers.

4. Findings

4.1 Retention Gains Replicate Tier 1 Field-Experiment Evidence

Across all 16 engineering sites (590 engineers), 12-month attrition averages 9.8% under 2-day hybrid versus 13.9% under 5-day office—a 29.0% relative reduction (95% bootstrap CI [-5.4 pp, -2.6 pp]) [1], [2] [strong]. Both senior (8.1% vs. 13.4%) and junior (11.6% vs. 14.3%) sites exhibit lower turnover [2]. This closely aligns with Bloom, Han & Liang's Nature RCT of 1,612 employees at Trip.com, which documented a 33% reduction in quit rates with zero penalty to performance grades or promotions [3] [strong].

4.2 Seniority Heterogeneity Falsifies Uniform Output Hypothesis (H₁)

Pooling all sites yields a modest -1.78 pp PR delta (-0.74% hybrid vs. +1.04% office, MAD 3.65 pp), which conceals opposite-signed subgroup effects [2] [moderate]:

Because senior and junior bootstrap 95% CIs are strictly opposite in sign and non-overlapping, H₁ (uniform output effect) is falsified in favor of H₂ [2] [strong].

4.3 Mentoring Proximity as the Primary Mechanism

Junior hybrid sites receive 1.73 mentoring hours/week versus 5.15 hours/week in 5-day office sites (r = +0.999 between mentoring hours and junior PR delta) [1], [2] [moderate]. This directly mirrors Emanuel, Harrington & Pallais's software-engineering proximity study, which found that co-location increases online code-review feedback to junior engineers by 18.3% while taxing senior engineers' immediate coding output [4] [strong].

5. Counter-Evidence & Alternative Explanations

  • PR Count Is an Incomplete Output Proxy: Merging more pull requests does not capture architectural quality, incident rates, or code-review load; senior engineers in hybrid sites may merge more PRs partly because they spend fewer hours reviewing junior code [4] [moderate].
  • Site-Level Aggregation & Small Site Count (n = 16): Without engineer-level microdata, site-level differences could reflect unobserved project complexity or manager quality across sites [1] [moderate].
  • Full-Remote Extrapolation Risk: Neither the local dataset nor the Trip.com Nature RCT directly evaluates a mandatory 5-day full-remote policy; extrapolating hybrid retention gains to full-remote without structured onboarding risks steeper junior skill-acquisition penalties [3], [4] [preliminary].

7. Limitations & Open Questions

  • Verified Methodological & Data Limitations: Site-level aggregates (n = 16 sites, 590 engineers) lack individual tenure, defect-severity weights, and a 5-day full-remote arm (both gaps logged in appendix/data_attempts.csv).
  • Actionable Next Steps / Experiments: Adopt 2-day hybrid with synchronized in-office "anchor days" (e.g., Tuesday–Thursday) pairing junior and senior engineers, track defect-weighted PR quality and code-review turnaround at the engineer level, and test whether scheduled virtual office hours recover junior mentoring hours.

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Reproducible Level 2 hybrid-work subgroup & mentoring audit emitting output.txt, charts.json v2, and numbers.json."""

from __future__ import annotations

import argparse
import json
import math
import random
import statistics
import sys
from pathlib import Path

ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[1]
SCRIPTS_DIR = ROOT.parents[1] / "skills" / "heurisma" / "scripts"
sys.path.insert(0, str(SCRIPTS_DIR))
from local_data import read_table  # noqa: E402


def median_abs_deviation(vals: list[float]) -> tuple[float, float]:
    med = statistics.median(vals)
    mad = statistics.median([abs(v - med) for v in vals])
    return mad, 1.4826 * mad


def pearson_r(xs: list[float], ys: list[float]) -> float:
    mx, my = statistics.fmean(xs), statistics.fmean(ys)
    num = sum((a - mx) * (b - my) for a, b in zip(xs, ys))
    den = math.sqrt(sum((a - mx) ** 2 for a in xs) * sum((b - my) ** 2 for b in ys))
    return num / den if den else 0.0


def bootstrap_diff_ci(a: list[float], b: list[float], n_boot: int = 2000, seed: int = 42) -> tuple[float, float]:
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    na, nb = len(a), len(b)
    draws = []
    for _ in range(n_boot):
        ma = statistics.fmean(a[rng.randrange(na)] for _ in range(na))
        mb = statistics.fmean(b[rng.randrange(nb)] for _ in range(nb))
        draws.append(ma - mb)
    draws.sort()
    return draws[int(0.025 * n_boot)], draws[int(0.975 * n_boot)]


def summarize(rows: list[dict], key: str) -> dict[str, float]:
    vals = [float(r[key]) for r in rows]
    mad, madn = median_abs_deviation(vals)
    return {
        "n": len(vals),
        "mean": statistics.fmean(vals),
        "median": statistics.median(vals),
        "sd": statistics.stdev(vals) if len(vals) > 1 else 0.0,
        "mad": mad,
        "madn": madn,
    }


def main() -> None:
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description="Analyze local hybrid_rct_sites.csv and emit charts.json + numbers.json.")
    parser.add_argument("--charts-out", type=Path, default=None, help="Output path for charts.json v2.")
    parser.add_argument("--numbers-out", type=Path, default=None, help="Output path for appendix/numbers.json.")
    args = parser.parse_args()

    rows = read_table(ROOT / "appendix" / "data" / "hybrid_rct_sites.csv")
    total_eng = sum(int(r["n_engineers"]) for r in rows)

    hyb = [r for r in rows if r["policy_group"] == "Hybrid_2d"]
    off = [r for r in rows if r["policy_group"] == "Office_5d"]
    sen_h = [r for r in hyb if r["seniority"] == "Senior"]
    sen_o = [r for r in off if r["seniority"] == "Senior"]
    jun_h = [r for r in hyb if r["seniority"] == "Junior"]
    jun_o = [r for r in off if r["seniority"] == "Junior"]

    # Output delta (PRs merged %)
    s_all_h, s_all_o = summarize(hyb, "prs_merged_delta_pct"), summarize(off, "prs_merged_delta_pct")
    s_sen_h, s_sen_o = summarize(sen_h, "prs_merged_delta_pct"), summarize(sen_o, "prs_merged_delta_pct")
    s_jun_h, s_jun_o = summarize(jun_h, "prs_merged_delta_pct"), summarize(jun_o, "prs_merged_delta_pct")

    # 12m attrition
    a_all_h, a_all_o = summarize(hyb, "attrition_12m_pct"), summarize(off, "attrition_12m_pct")
    a_sen_h, a_sen_o = summarize(sen_h, "attrition_12m_pct"), summarize(sen_o, "attrition_12m_pct")
    a_jun_h, a_jun_o = summarize(jun_h, "attrition_12m_pct"), summarize(jun_o, "attrition_12m_pct")

    # Mentoring hours/wk
    m_jun_h = summarize(jun_h, "mentoring_hrs_wk")["mean"]
    m_jun_o = summarize(jun_o, "mentoring_hrs_wk")["mean"]

    # Differences & bootstrap 95% CIs
    diff_pr_all = s_all_h["mean"] - s_all_o["mean"]
    diff_pr_sen = s_sen_h["mean"] - s_sen_o["mean"]
    diff_pr_jun = s_jun_h["mean"] - s_jun_o["mean"]
    ci_pr_sen = bootstrap_diff_ci([r["prs_merged_delta_pct"] for r in sen_h], [r["prs_merged_delta_pct"] for r in sen_o])
    ci_pr_jun = bootstrap_diff_ci([r["prs_merged_delta_pct"] for r in jun_h], [r["prs_merged_delta_pct"] for r in jun_o])

    rel_attr_drop = (a_all_h["mean"] - a_all_o["mean"]) / a_all_o["mean"]
    diff_attr_pp = (a_all_h["mean"] - a_all_o["mean"]) * 100.0
    ci_attr_all = bootstrap_diff_ci([r["attrition_12m_pct"] for r in hyb], [r["attrition_12m_pct"] for r in off])

    # Mentoring vs PR delta correlation across all 16 sites
    ment_xs = [float(r["mentoring_hrs_wk"]) for r in rows]
    pr_ys = [float(r["prs_merged_delta_pct"]) for r in rows]
    r_ment_pr = pearson_r(ment_xs, pr_ys)
    r_ment_jun = pearson_r([float(r["mentoring_hrs_wk"]) for r in jun_h + jun_o], [float(r["prs_merged_delta_pct"]) for r in jun_h + jun_o])

    print(f"Dataset integrity: sites={len(rows)}, total_engineers={total_eng}, missing=0")
    print(f"Pooled PR delta: Hybrid_2d={s_all_h['mean']*100:+.2f}% (MAD={s_all_h['mad']*100:.2f}pp) vs Office_5d={s_all_o['mean']*100:+.2f}% (diff={diff_pr_all*100:+.2f}pp)")
    print(f"Senior PR delta: Hybrid_2d={s_sen_h['mean']*100:+.2f}% vs Office_5d={s_sen_o['mean']*100:+.2f}% (diff={diff_pr_sen*100:+.2f}pp, 95% boot CI [{ci_pr_sen[0]*100:+.2f}pp, {ci_pr_sen[1]*100:+.2f}pp])")
    print(f"Junior PR delta: Hybrid_2d={s_jun_h['mean']*100:+.2f}% vs Office_5d={s_jun_o['mean']*100:+.2f}% (diff={diff_pr_jun*100:+.2f}pp, 95% boot CI [{ci_pr_jun[0]*100:+.2f}pp, {ci_pr_jun[1]*100:+.2f}pp])")
    print(f"12m Attrition: Hybrid_2d={a_all_h['mean']*100:.1f}% vs Office_5d={a_all_o['mean']*100:.1f}% (diff={diff_attr_pp:+.1f}pp, rel={rel_attr_drop*100:+.1f}%, 95% boot CI [{ci_attr_all[0]*100:+.1f}pp, {ci_attr_all[1]*100:+.1f}pp])")
    print(f"Senior Attrition: Hybrid_2d={a_sen_h['mean']*100:.1f}% vs Office_5d={a_sen_o['mean']*100:.1f}%")
    print(f"Junior Attrition: Hybrid_2d={a_jun_h['mean']*100:.1f}% vs Office_5d={a_jun_o['mean']*100:.1f}%")
    print(f"Junior mentoring hrs/wk: Hybrid_2d={m_jun_h:.2f} vs Office_5d={m_jun_o:.2f} (r_all={r_ment_pr:+.3f}, r_junior={r_ment_jun:+.3f})")

    if args.charts_out is not None:
        worst_jun = min(jun_h, key=lambda r: float(r["prs_merged_delta_pct"]))
        charts_v2 = [

                "id": "subgroup_output",

                    {"name": "Office (5d)", "values": [round(s_all_o["mean"], 4), round(s_sen_o["mean"], 4), round(s_jun_o["mean"], 4)]},
