# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating intellectual honesty, methodological rigor, and constructive skepticism.
- Giving and gracefully accepting evidence-based feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the scientific integrity of the skill.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or insulting comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting fabricated evaluation results or unverified claims in skill tests.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported via GitHub Issues or by contacting the repository maintainer (`@777aeterna`). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
